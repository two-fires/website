The Intelligence Suite is a set of agents that do senior strategy work on your business using real public evidence, not guesswork. Module 1 is the diagnosis. The four stages

Each stage reads the ones before it. Run them in order and the work compounds. You can leave and come back: every completed stage is saved against your login with a downloadable PDF.

What you get at the end

  • Four reports, each downloadable as a branded PDF, each with its evidence and confidence level shown.
  • An executive summary on the Communications Audit: your top comms issues, your strengths, and what to prioritise.
  • A marketing scorecard rating your positioning, messaging, channels and offer.
  • A prioritised action plan with quick wins, drawn from all three upstream stages rather than one tool's opinion.
  • Your Diagnosis Hub, one page that holds every run and every version so you can see how the picture changed.
