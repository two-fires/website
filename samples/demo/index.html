About Us

Northline Physio was established in 2014 with a simple mission: to provide high quality, patient-centred physiotherapy care to the Northern Beaches community.

Our modern, fully equipped clinic is conveniently located in the heart of Dee Why, with parking available. We pride ourselves on our friendly, professional approach and our commitment to achieving the best possible outcomes for every patient.

Whether you are recovering from an injury, managing a chronic condition, or simply want to move better, we are here to help.
